What we offer

Everything a farm needs to go organic

From bio-inputs to certification training, we work alongside farmers and processors at every stage of the switch to organic.

🌱

Organic fertilisers & bio-inputs

Sourcing and supply of organic manures, bio-fertilisers and bio-pesticides suited to local soil and crop conditions.

🧪

Soil & input testing

Basic soil health checks and input-quality guidance so farmers apply the right product, at the right time, in the right quantity.

📋

Certification support

Guidance for organic processors and handlers through recognised external training needed to meet organic certification standards.

🤝

Farmer advisory

On-ground consulting on crop planning, input schedules and the practical steps of transitioning from chemical to organic farming.

📦

Sales & distribution

Reliable supply and distribution of organic farming products to growers and processors across the region.

🌍

Market linkage

Helping certified organic produce and processors connect with buyers who value recognised, sustainable standards.
